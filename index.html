Meshwarna ("App"), developed by Ahmed Adel ("we", "our", or "us"), is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Full name and phone number

b. Location Data

  • Precise location for trip matching and live tracking

c. Messages

  • Messages shared within trip group chats

d. Technical Data

  • Device type and OS version

3. How We Use Data

4. Data Sharing

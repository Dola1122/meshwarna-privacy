Privacy Policy

Meshwarna – Daily Ride Sharing App

Developed by Ahmed Adel

Effective Date: March 2026

1. Introduction

Meshwarna ("App", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Profile details (student/employee, gender, ratings, subscription status)

b. Location Data

  • Precise location for trip matching and live tracking
  • Approximate location for improving suggestions

c. Messages

  • Messages within trip group chats
  • May be reviewed temporarily for safety and misuse prevention

d. Technical Data

  • Device type and OS version
  • Usage analytics and crash logs

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Match users into trips
  • Enable communication between users
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Detect fraud and enforce rules

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • User consent (account creation, location access)
  • Providing the service (ride matching)
  • Legitimate interest (safety and fraud prevention)

5. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • Users within the same trip
  • Service providers (analytics, hosting)

We do NOT sell your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Restricted data access

8. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your account:

  • From inside the app (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Or by contacting us via email

10. Sign in with Apple

When using Sign in with Apple, we only collect the information provided by Apple (such as name and email). We do not track users across apps.

11. Children

This app is not intended for users under 18.

12. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services like Firebase for analytics and authentication.

13. Updates

We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance of changes.

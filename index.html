Privacy Policy for Meshwarna – Daily Ride Sharing App

Effective Date: March 2026

1. Introduction

Meshwarna (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when using the Meshwarna mobile application (“App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Full name, phone number
  • Profile details including affiliation (student/employee), gender, ratings, and subscription status

b. Location Information

  • Precise location for route matching and live tracking during trips
  • Approximate location for suggestions and hotspot identification

c. Messages

  • Messages sent within group chats for trip coordination
  • Note: We may access these messages temporarily to ensure compliance with our guidelines and monitor inappropriate content.

e. App Performance & Usage Data

  • Device type, OS version, crash logs, app usage statistics for performance improvement

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Functionality: To allow core features of the App (trip creation, joining groups, OTP login, etc.)
  • Account Management: To manage user accounts, subscription status, and user profiles
  • Communication: To facilitate group chats and coordination among users
  • Safety & Fraud Prevention: To detect suspicious activity, ensure user safety, and enforce rules
  • Analytics & Performance Improvement: To improve App performance, troubleshoot errors, and enhance user experience
  • Monitoring Chat Content: We may review messages sent within groups to ensure appropriate conduct

4. Data Sharing

  • Shared only with:
    • Other users in the same trip/group
    • Payment providers when a transaction is made
    • Authorized service providers for analytics or App performance
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

  • All sensitive data is encrypted during transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only

6. User Choices

  • Users cannot opt out of providing personal information required for account creation and trip functionality
  • Users may disable optional analytics data collection through App settings (if available)
